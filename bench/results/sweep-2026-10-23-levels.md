[`benchreport`](../cmd/benchreport) from its JSON: a table for each scenario and protocol, a row for
each server, ranked by successful responses a second (TPS), then CPU efficiency, then memory
efficiency, each followed by its share of the best. A run with no result says why: **hung** (the
load generator did not finish and was ended after 300 s), or **no responses** in its Errors column
(the load generator finished with none answered). HTTP/1.1 runs have the level's connections;
h2c, h2 and HTTP/3 runs its connections of 32 streams each.
