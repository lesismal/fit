# Load test plan derived from the machine, 2026-10-24: the tables

The report: [English](report.md) | [简体中文](report.zh-CN.md)

The tables of every workload of [the load test plan](../../README.md#the-load-test-plan), as benchreport
renders them from the JSON in this folder: 232 runs on the benchmark host, 2026-10-24 14:51 to
16:23 UTC, fit at e5c2941 and the harness at c8ee767, every run 5s warm-up and 15s measured, none
failed, none hung, no errors in any. The plan's sizes were derived from the machine by
`fitbench plan` ([plan.txt](plan.txt)) and came out as the hand-tuned plan's: levels 100 to 10,000
requests in flight; the server under test on CPUs `0-1,8-9` (4 workers), the backends on
`2-4,10-12`, the load generators on `5-7,13-15`; W6 calibrated to 20,000 and 100,000 a second. A
cell is the successful responses a second and their share of the best.

The backends never set the pace: the proxy backend used at most 2.18 of its 6 CPUs (nginx's
HTTP/1.1 proxy upload, W4), at most 2.00 in W1, and the cache origin at most 0.01. Every server
under test used all of its 4 CPUs at every level but W6's.

Every table has the servers' memory: peak anonymous memory during the run, and MEM EER, successful
responses a second for each MiB of it; the scaling tables add peak memory at each level.
