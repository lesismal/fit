Calls, publishing and playing over fit's WebRTC media server.

Call

A room call: everyone publishes their camera and microphone and plays the others. Switch either off at any time; the others see it.

Publish

Send a camera, a microphone or a screen to a stream with WHIP, and get the stream's URLs for other tools.

Play

Play a stream with WHEP, or HLS where the browser plays it.
